# Covering emails

**INTERNAL. Not a client document.** This file is deliberately outside
`reporting/content/`, which holds only client-facing copy and is read raw by
`reporting/loadDraftReport.ts` and built into PDFs by `reporting/pdf/build.sh`.
Nothing here is built into anything. The consultant copies the text.

Both are short on purpose. A welcome email that runs to a screen and a half does not
get read, and the pack it is introducing then does not get opened either. Figures are
written out rather than tokenised because these are pasted into a mail client, not
rendered by `render.py`. If the turnaround or the price changes, change it here too.

---

## 1. On sign-up: asking for their roles

> Subject: Your Zigbert set-up, and the one thing we need from you

Hi [name],

Good to have you on board. There is one thing we need before we can build your
dashboard, and then the rest is us.

Attached is the **Zigbert Benchmarking Template**. It asks for five things about each
role you want benchmarked: the job title, the current full-time-equivalent salary, the
experience level, the function, and the location. There is a tab for your organisation
too, which is four fields and only needed once.

Fill in the highlighted cells and email it back. Within 48 hours of it landing, your
dashboard is built, reviewed by one of our reward specialists, and ready for you.

If any role sits awkwardly between two experience levels, leave a note in the row
rather than guessing. We would rather look at it.

Anything unclear, just reply to this.

[sign-off]

## 2. At handover: the dashboard is ready

> Subject: Your Zigbert dashboard is ready

Hi [name],

Your dashboard is built and reviewed. Everything you need to start is attached, and
your login details follow in a separate email.

**Watch the 60-second film first.** It shows you what is in there faster than I can
describe it. After that, the welcome pack opens with a page on what each attachment is
and the order to use them, so you do not have to open all of them today.

Two things worth doing in your first week:

- Check your own roles on **Your Organisation**. Everything in the dashboard is
  calculated from that list, so it is worth five minutes.
- Look at the roles sitting below the market median. That is where acting early costs
  least.

One of the attachments is a board paper template. It is there because the dashboard can
tell you what a pay review would cost but cannot make the case for it in your
organisation, and that is usually the harder half.

I am on the end of an email whenever you want a second opinion on a number. Happy to
walk through any of it on a call in a couple of weeks once you have had a look.

[sign-off]
