## Your data

You are giving us salary information about identifiable people. That deserves a straight answer about where it goes, so here it is.

### What we hold

Three things, and nothing else.

**Your roles and their salaries.** The job titles, functions, levels, locations and pay you send us or enter in your dashboard. Where you use the By person view, this includes individual salaries and FTE.

**Your benefits.** What you provide, to whom, and at what level.

**Your account.** The named people at your organisation who have access, and their contact details.

We do not ask for and do not want employee names, national insurance numbers, dates of birth, addresses, performance ratings or protected characteristics. Nothing in the dashboard needs them. If a spreadsheet you send us happens to contain a column like that, we delete the column rather than load it, and we will tell you we have done so.

### Where it is held

Your data is held in a managed Postgres database provided by Supabase, hosted in [[TO CONFIRM: hosting region, e.g. the EU (Ireland)]]. It is encrypted in transit and at rest. It is not held in your browser, so it is not lost if you clear your cache and it follows you to another device.

Each client's data is separated by row-level security, so a query made by one client's account cannot return another client's rows. Access from our side is limited to [[TO CONFIRM: who at TwentySix can access client data, e.g. named reward specialists and one system administrator]].

### Who sees it

Your named users, and the TwentySix reward specialist reviewing your dashboard. Nobody else.

Your figures are never shown to another client, never published, and never used to advertise. They do not feed our market dataset: that is built from job adverts and survey evidence, which is a separate body of data described in the Methodology page of your dashboard. Your salaries improve nothing except your own benchmarks.

## Your rights and our obligations

For the purposes of UK GDPR, **you are the data controller** for the employee pay data you send us and **TwentySix Consulting is the data processor**. We act on your instructions, we do not decide what to do with it, and we do not use it for our own purposes.

That relationship is set out in the data processing agreement accompanying your contract. It covers the security measures, our sub-processors, breach notification, and what happens at the end of the engagement.

### How long we keep it

We hold your data for the life of your subscription. [[TO CONFIRM: retention period after a subscription ends, e.g. deleted within 30 days of termination unless you ask us to retain it]].

You can ask us at any time to export everything we hold for you, correct something that is wrong, or delete it. Email your consultant and we will do it. There is no charge and no notice period.

### Two things worth telling your team

**A pay benchmarking exercise is usually a legitimate interest rather than something needing consent**, but that is your assessment to make and record, not ours. If it helps, we can describe what we do in writing for your privacy notice.

**Individual salaries are visible to whoever has your dashboard login.** That is normally the right people, and it is worth being deliberate about it. Access is per named user and we add or remove people the same working day you ask.
