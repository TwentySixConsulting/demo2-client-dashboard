## How this works, start to finish

<p class="hw-intro">Ten steps from agreeing scope to a dashboard that keeps itself
current. Four of them are yours, and three of those happen once.</p>

<p class="hw-phase">Getting set up</p>

<div class="hw-list">
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">1</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Agree what you want benchmarked.</b> How many roles, and the price that goes with them.</span>
    <span class="hw-who both">Together</span>
    <span class="hw-when">&mdash;</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">2</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Fill in the spreadsheet we send you.</b> Five things per role. The next page lists them.</span>
    <span class="hw-who you">You</span>
    <span class="hw-when">&mdash;</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">3</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Email it back.</b> That is the last thing we need from you before your dashboard exists.</span>
    <span class="hw-who you">You</span>
    <span class="hw-when">&mdash;</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">4</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>We classify and benchmark every role</b> against {salary_records} UK salary records, matched on function, level, industry and location.</span>
    <span class="hw-who us">Us</span>
    <span class="hw-when">{turnaround}</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">5</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>A reward specialist checks the result</b> and signs it off. Nobody receives a dashboard we have not reviewed.</span>
    <span class="hw-who us">Us</span>
    <span class="hw-when">same window</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">6</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Your dashboard, your login and this pack arrive.</b> Nothing to install, configure or integrate.</span>
    <span class="hw-who us">Us</span>
    <span class="hw-when">&mdash;</span>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="hw-phase">Your first week</p>

<div class="hw-list">
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">7</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Check your own roster is right.</b> On Your Organisation. Everything else is calculated from it.</span>
    <span class="hw-who you">You</span>
    <span class="hw-when">15 minutes</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">8</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Take the tours, then look at the roles below the median.</b> That is where acting early is cheapest.</span>
    <span class="hw-who you">You</span>
    <span class="hw-when">half an hour</span>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="hw-phase">From then on</p>

<div class="hw-list">
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">9</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>Add a role whenever you need one.</b> It goes to your consultant and comes back benchmarked.</span>
    <span class="hw-who both">You, then us</span>
    <span class="hw-when">{turnaround} each</span>
  </div>
  <div class="hw-row">
    <span class="hw-n">10</span>
    <span class="hw-what"><b>The market data refreshes every month.</b> Your dashboard is rebuilt from it, reviewed and republished.</span>
    <span class="hw-who us">Us</span>
    <span class="hw-when">quarterly</span>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="note">Steps 4 and 5 are the ones that matter most and the ones you never see.
Classification is the hard part of benchmarking from advert data, and the specialist
review is why a Zigbert dashboard is not a self-service search result.</p>
