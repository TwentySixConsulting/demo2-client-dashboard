## Market context

This lives on the **Trends and Hotspots** page of the dashboard (`/trends`), which carries the
economic indicators, the CPI and unemployment series, wage floors, the 2026 pay-rise landscape,
role-level pay pressure and the benefits market direction.

Every figure there is derived rather than typed: each KPI tile reads the last point of its own
series, so a tile and its chart cannot disagree. Restating any of it in this file would
reintroduce exactly the drift that design prevents.
