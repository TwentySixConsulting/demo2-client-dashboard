## Where the data comes from

This is covered in full, and kept current, on the **Methodology** page of the dashboard
(`/methodology`). It is deliberately not duplicated here: the page interpolates every figure
from `client/src/lib/theme.ts`, so a sample size or a refresh date cannot go stale on one
surface and not the other. A copy in this file would be the surface that goes stale.

Its nine sections cover where the pay numbers come from, how records are cleaned and
classified, the four dimensions that define a comparator group, the official data behind the
economic context, how benefits are benchmarked differently from pay, the specialist review,
the refresh cadence, how to read what you are given, and when to ask for bespoke work.

If a client asks for provenance in writing, send them there rather than writing it out again.
